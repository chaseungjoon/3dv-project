- The paper's own ParaHome success definition is "accurately reproducing the demonstrated interaction", measured in
  the released code by the lenient `place_metric` (= `paper_success` here: book above 0.9 m near a wrist for > 60
  frames). The strict per-frame 10 cm `task_success` is this project's definition. Which one the 70% target refers to
  is the team's decision (protocol.json `target`).
